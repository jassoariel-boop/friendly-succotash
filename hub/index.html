Rotate one reading format per week

  • Echo reading: Coach reads a sentence, students repeat it. Best for Beginner.
  • Popcorn reading: students take turns reading one sentence each.
  • Partner reading: pairs trade paragraphs.

Three bolded vocabulary words per passage. Say each word, hear a kid-friendly definition, spot it in the reading. End with one comprehension question.

Tablet research weeks replace the passage with 10 minutes of guided lookup. Pairs find two facts, write them on a sticky note, and use at least one in their build. Keep tablets on a bookmarked kid-safe search page.

Builder Card

Every competition entry gets an index card. Cards stay with the build for judging and the Week 8 gallery walk. Beginners can draw and label, or dictate to a helper.
