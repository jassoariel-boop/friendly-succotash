Teaching by group

  • Challenge tiers. Every challenge card has three tiers. Everyone starts on Tier 1 (Beginner). A builder who finishes and passes the test moves to Tier 2, then Tier 3. Tiers follow skill, not grade, so a strong 2nd grader can reach Tier 3.
  • Grades 1 and 2. Read the passage to them and have them echo it. Builder Cards can be drawn and labeled. Give a 2-minute warning before every switch.
  • Grades 3 to 5. Students read aloud in popcorn style and write full Builder Card sentences. Give them the measuring jobs.
  • Seniors. Each lesson ends with a seniors note. Run it as 60 minutes in small teams of 2 or 3, with a real-world talking point. Skip prizes unless the group enjoys them.
  • Memory care (45 min). One step at a time, larger bricks if you have them, and no timer.
